Our story

Three generations, one oven that's never gone cold.

John started with a single deck oven and a folding table out front. His son took over the sauce recipe — and refused to change a single ingredient. Now his granddaughter runs the counter, and the dough still gets made the same way it always has: by hand, every morning, before the sun's fully up.

We're not trying to reinvent pizza. We're just trying to make the kind you'd drive across town for — crisp on the outside, soft in the middle, and gone before you meant it to be.
